Mandarin Starter Watchlist for Bilingual Families (Ages 3–6)

Start with one collection. Greetings and familiar routines help children get started in Mandarin. Try the animated stories once short phrases are familiar.

Preview and save the Mandarin watchlist

On a phone, open the preview in your browser, set or enter the parent PIN, then save to this device. YouTube needs no Domi helper. Playback depends on your region and YouTube’s restrictions, and the player may show ads or request sign-in.

Three Mandarin Learning Collections for Ages 3–6

Each collection is arranged in a suggested learning order. Pace and level are editorial guides. YouTube closed captions were unavailable when checked.

Songs to Sing Along

6 episodes · Beginner · slow to moderate. Start with greetings and goodbyes, then sing about numbers, colors and weather.

  1. Hello! · 1:03 · Little Fox Chinese
  2. Good-bye! · 1:06 · Little Fox Chinese
  3. My Family · 1:53 · Little Fox Chinese
  4. Number Song · 0:55 · Little Fox Chinese
  5. Colors Song · 1:44 · Little Fox Chinese
  6. How’s the Weather? · 1:09 · Little Fox Chinese

Mandarin for Everyday Routines

3 episodes · Beginner · moderate. Practice saying what you are doing as you wash hands or tidy up.

  1. I Can Wash My Hands · 3:59 · LiaChaCha 依娜和恰恰
  2. I Can Tidy Up My Toys · 3:02 · LiaChaCha 依娜和恰恰
  3. First Trip to the Park · 3:55 · LiaChaCha 依娜和恰恰

Little Fox Animated Stories

3 episodes · Next step · moderate. Start with the shorter Fox and the Crow, then retell the ending in a sentence or two.

  1. The Fox and the Crow · 3:33 · Little Fox Chinese
  2. The Tortoise and the Hare · 4:38 · Little Fox Chinese
  3. The Three Little Pigs · 7:58 · Little Fox Chinese

One thing to try after watching

Say “洗手” together while washing hands. Ask for a toy of a particular color when tidying up. Once the familiar words are easy, retell the race in The Tortoise and the Hare together.
