How to use this list with a toddler

  • Pick one collection for the moment. Move and Sing suits active time, the routine songs suit getting dressed or bath time, and the bedtime songs suit the last few minutes before sleep.
  • Watch or sing along together when you can. Many toddlers enjoy the actions more when an adult joins in.
  • Keep sessions short. The World Health Organization suggests no more than one hour of sedentary screen time a day for children aged 2 to 4, and says less is better. (WHO, 2019)
  • In Domi Lists, a parent can set a daily watch-time limit.

Things to watch for

The songs are in simple, slow English, and the Bluey episodes use Australian English. They play in YouTube's own player, which may show ads or platform controls. A video that plays in one country may not play in another, or it may need a signed-in account. If a link fails, skip that video or report it below.

You decide whether these videos suit your child. Some 2-year-olds will enjoy only the songs at first. The Bluey episodes are longer and tell a small story, which may suit children closer to 3 or 4.

After you save

Saving puts a copy of this list on your device. You can add or remove videos, then let your child watch in kids mode, which shows only the videos you saved. The parent area is protected by a PIN you set. No account is required. Each collection plays in order.

If you already saved the English starter list for ages 3 to 6, Walking in the Jungle is already in your library, so saving this list adds the other 60 videos.

Frequently asked questions

What should a 2 year old watch on YouTube?

Short videos with simple words, songs they can move to, and calm stories work well for many 2-year-olds. This list uses action songs, nursery rhymes and routine songs from Super Simple Songs, plus full Bluey episodes of about 7 minutes. You know your child best, so remove anything that does not suit them.

How long are the videos?

Most songs last 1 to 4 minutes. Each Bluey episode is about 7 minutes. The whole list is about 3 hours 45 minutes, split into 4 collections so you can play one part at a time.

Do I need an account to use this list?

No. Domi Lists is free and does not require an account. Saving keeps the list in your browser on this device.

Can I add or remove videos?

Yes. After you save the list, open the parent area to remove videos or paste in your own YouTube or Bilibili links.

Will there be ads?

Videos play in YouTube's own player, which may show ads or platform controls.
