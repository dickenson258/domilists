Why watch a read-aloud

A read-aloud video keeps the book at the centre: you see the pages and hear the whole story at a calm pace. Many families use one as a quiet end to the day, or as a way to meet a book before borrowing it from the library. It works best when you watch together and talk about the story afterwards.

How to use this list

  • Start with the shorter collection for younger children or for bedtime.
  • Move to the longer collection when your child can follow a story for 8 to 12 minutes.
  • Pause and ask a question or two: what might happen next, or how a character feels.
  • If your child likes a book, look for the printed copy at your library.

Things to watch for

The stories are read in natural, unhurried English. Some folk tales have a mild scare, such as the troll in The Three Billy Goats Gruff or the wolf in The Three Little Pigs and the Somewhat Bad Wolf. Watch one first if your child is sensitive.

The videos play in YouTube's own player, which may show ads or platform controls. A video that plays in one country may not play in another. If a link fails, skip it or report it below.

Frequently asked questions

What is Storyline Online?

Storyline Online is a children's literacy program from the SAG-AFTRA Foundation. Actors read picture books aloud, and the video shows the book's illustrations. The videos are free to watch on YouTube.

How long are the read-alouds?

In this list, the shorter stories take 4 to 7 minutes and the longer ones take 7 to 12 minutes. All 22 books together take about 2 hours 46 minutes.

What age are these books for?

Most of these books are picture books for preschool and early primary school, roughly ages 3 to 7. You know your child best, so start with the shorter collection and see how it goes.

Is a read-aloud video a replacement for reading together?

It works well alongside reading together. Many parents find that watching with their child and talking about the story afterwards makes it feel closer to shared reading time.

Do I need an account?

No. Domi Lists is free and does not require an account. The list is saved in your browser on this device.
