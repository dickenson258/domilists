An English Starter Watchlist for Ages 3–6: Seven Collections and a Song

This is an English watchlist for children aged 3 to 6: 116 YouTube videos in 7 collections and 1 song, about 9 hours 48 minutes in all. It covers numbers with Numberblocks, letters and phonics with Alphablocks, first coding ideas with Wonderblocks, everyday science with Maddie's Do You Know? and SciShow Kids, and picture books read aloud by Storyline Online. You decide whether the age range and English level are right for your child. Free to use, no account needed. Optional family sync will be a paid feature later. You can add or remove videos after you save.

Things to watch for

The videos are spoken English at a preschool pace. Some science words in the SciShow Kids collections may be new to a beginner.

They play in YouTube's own player, which may show ads or platform controls. A video that plays in one country may not play in another, or it may need a signed-in account. If a link fails, skip that video or report it below.

You can stop at any time. In Domi Lists, a parent can set a daily watch-time limit. After you save, each collection plays in episode order.

After you save

Saving puts a copy of this list on your device. You can add or remove videos, then let your child watch in kids mode. The parent area is protected by a PIN you set. No account is required.

Frequently asked questions

What is in the English starter watchlist?

116 YouTube videos from official channels in 7 collections and 1 song: Numberblocks (15), Alphablocks (17), Wonderblocks (20), Maddie's Do You Know? (18), SciShow Kids garden science (16) and weather (11), Storyline Online read-alouds (18), and the song Walking in the Jungle.

Is it suitable for a child who is just starting English?

The videos use spoken English at a preschool pace, and Numberblocks and Alphablocks show a lot visually. Some science words in the SciShow Kids collections may be new to a beginner. You decide what fits your child, and you can remove any collection after saving.

How long are the episodes?

Most are 3 to 7 minutes. The Storyline Online read-alouds run about 5 to 12 minutes. The whole list is about 9 hours 48 minutes, so it is meant to be watched a little at a time.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

Free to use, no account needed. Optional family sync will be a paid feature later. Saving puts a copy of the list in your browser on this device.

Can I change the list after saving it?

Yes. In the parent area you can remove videos, reorder or rename collections, and paste in your own YouTube or Bilibili links.

I saved the earlier 5-video starter list. What happens if I save this one?

Videos you already have are skipped, so the rest of this list is added and nothing is duplicated. Videos you saved earlier stay where they were in your library and do not move into the new collections.
