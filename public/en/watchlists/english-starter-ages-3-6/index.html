An English Starter Watchlist for Ages 3–6: Five Videos to Try

This is a short YouTube list for families who want to try English-language videos with a child aged 3 to 6. It includes Numberblocks, Alphablocks, Wonderblocks, an episode of Maddie's Do You Know? about bubbles, and a short SciShow Kids episode. You decide whether the age range and English level are right for your child. You can save the list in Domi Lists and add or remove videos afterwards. No account is required.

Who this list is for

Suggested age: 3 to 6, based on the official series and episode topics (early numbers, letters, simple planning, and short kids' science). This is a suggestion, not a guarantee.

English level: mixed spoken English at a preschool pace. You may want to watch a minute or two yourself before you hand the list to your child.

Things to watch for

All five are spoken English at a preschool pace, and each one is under six minutes. Some science words in the last two episodes may be new to a beginner.

All five play in YouTube's own player, which may show ads or platform controls. A video that plays in one country may not play in another, or it may need a signed-in account. If a link fails, skip that video or report it (see below).

You can stop at any time. In Domi Lists, a parent can also set a daily watch-time limit.

After you save

Saving puts a copy of this list on your device. You can add or remove videos, then let your child watch in kids mode. The parent area is protected by a PIN you set. No account is required.
