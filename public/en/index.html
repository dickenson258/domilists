Good to know

Domi Lists, or 朵米片单 in Chinese, is a web app for parents to build and share video watchlists for their children. Free to use, no account needed. Optional family sync will be a paid feature later. Start with a sample list, save it on your device, and add or remove videos later.

YouTube and Bilibili work right away. Some other sites need an optional helper. Videos play in their original YouTube or Bilibili players, which may show ads or platform controls.

The site is lists.domibuddy.com.

Who it is for

Domi Lists is for parents of young children who want a short list their child can watch. It is a good fit if you already use YouTube or Bilibili, including English-speaking families and Chinese-speaking families living outside China.

You judge whether a list suits your child's age and English level. The starter list on this site is meant for ages 3 to 6.

How it works

  1. Open a starter list, or paste your own YouTube or Bilibili links in the parent area.
  2. Save the list on this device. You do not create an account. The parent area is protected by a PIN you set.
  3. Your child watches in kids mode, which shows only the videos you saved.
  4. You can add or remove videos whenever you want.

The list and settings stay in this browser unless you turn on family sync or share a list. Optional family sync will be a paid feature later.

You can also set a daily watch-time limit in the parent area.

Where is my list stored?

Your list and settings stay in this browser on this device. If you share a list, its video IDs, titles and collection names are stored on our server to make the short link. If you turn on family sync, the list is encrypted and uploaded so your other devices can use it.
